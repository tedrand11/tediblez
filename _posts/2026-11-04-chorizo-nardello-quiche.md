### Step-by-Step Instructions

#### 1. Blind Bake the Shell
Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Fit the pie crust into your tin or dish, crimping the edges. Dock the bottom with a fork a few times. Line with parchment paper, fill to the top with pie weights (or dried beans), and blind bake for 12–14 minutes until the edges are set and pale golden. Remove the weights and parchment.

#### 2. The Egg Wash Moisture Shield
Separate one of your eggs. Take the egg white and lightly beat it, then brush a thin coat across the warm bottom and sides of the crust. Return it to the oven for 2 minutes to cook and set. This forms a crisp hydrophobic barrier that prevents the dreaded soggy bottom. Reserve the leftover yolk for your custard.

#### 3. Crisp the Chorizo & Control Moisture
Heat a skillet over medium heat. Brown the 6 oz of chorizo, breaking it up into small crumbly bits. Once cooked through, transfer the chorizo to a paper towel-lined plate to thoroughly drain off excess surface fat and moisture (quiche enemy #1). Keep 1–2 tablespoons of the seasoned rendered fat in the pan.

#### 4. Caramelize the Onions & Peppers
Drop your thinly sliced onions into the remaining chorizo fat over medium-low heat. Sauté slowly with a small pinch of salt until translucent and deeply golden. Toss in the sliced Jimmy Nardello pepper rings and continue cooking for 3–5 minutes until the peppers are tender-crisp and sweet. Remove from heat and allow to cool slightly.

#### 5. Whisk the Velvety Custard
In a mixing bowl, whisk together the remaining 3 whole eggs, the 1 reserved egg yolk, 1/2 cup heavy cream, 1/2 cup milk, a pinch of paprika, a light pinch of salt, and freshly ground black pepper until completely smooth and frothy.

#### 6. Layer & Fill
Scatter **half** of the shredded extra sharp cheddar evenly across the bottom crust. Spread the drained chorizo, sweet caramelized onions, and Nardello pepper rings evenly over the cheese. Pour the egg-and-cream custard slowly over the fillings, then crown the top with the remaining half of the sharp cheddar.

#### 7. Bake, Cool, & Serve
Slide the quiche onto the center rack and bake at 350°F (175°C) for roughly 45–50 minutes, or until the edges are puffed and golden and the center has a gentle, subtle jiggle without being sloshy. 

Pull it from the oven and **let it sit on a wire rack for at least 30 minutes** before slicing so the custard finishes setting cleanly. Slice into wedges and douse generously with Valentina hot sauce.

---

### Cook's Notes

* **Moisture Management:** Searing down the chorizo and drying it thoroughly on paper towels keeps the quiche structure rich and custard-like rather than greasy.
* **Why Jimmy Nardellos?** Jimmy Nardello peppers look like spicy chilis but are completely sweet, thin-skinned, and fruity, making them the ultimate pairing with salty, spicy Mexican chorizo.
