### Instructions

1. **Coat the Blueberries**: In a plastic bag, add 2 tbsp flour, 1 tsp sugar, and the blueberries. Shake until the blueberries are fully coated.
2. **Whisk Dry Ingredients**: In one bowl, add the 3 cups flour, baking powder, and salt, and stir to combine.
3. **Cream Wet Ingredients**: In another bowl, add the sugar, eggs, milk, and butter, and stir it into a cream.
4. **Combine in Batches**: Mix 1/3 of the flour mixture with 1/2 of the milk mixture. Then add another 1/3 flour mix, the remaining 1/2 milk mix, and the final 1/3 flour mix, stirring continuously as you go.
5. **Fold Berries**: Add the coated blueberries and stir rapidly to distribute evenly.
