### Instructions

1. **Aromatics & Beef**: Simmer onion while separately browning beef and draining the grease.
2. **Brown the Rice**: Add rice, seasonings, and pepperoncini juice and brown.
3. **Add Broth & Meat**: Slowly add in beef broth. Stir in beef, and add pepperoncini on top.
4. **Settle & Fold**: Then fold 'em in once it settles. Finish with Tobasco to ur likin'.

---

### Cook's Notes

* **The Pepperoncini Juice:** Pouring the brine directly into the dry rice infuses the grains with acid and salt before the broth is added.
* **Toasting the Grains:** Browning the dry rice in the seasoned base keeps the grains firm and flavorful as they simmer.
