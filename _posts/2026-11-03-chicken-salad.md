### Step-by-Step Instructions

#### 1. Whip the Creamy Dressing Base
In a medium mixing bowl, beat the 4 oz of softened cream cheese with a fork or small whisk until smooth and pliable. Whisk in the 1/2 cup mayonnaise, 1 tsp Dijon mustard, 1/2 tsp garlic powder, and 1/4 tsp onion powder until fully incorporated and velvety with no cream cheese lumps. Season with a pinch of kosher salt and freshly cracked black pepper to taste.

#### 2. Prep & Toss the Savory-Sweet Mix-Ins
In a separate large bowl, combine the 4 cups of diced rotisserie chicken (a mix of breast and dark meat gives the best texture), the quartered red grapes, finely diced celery stalks, and thinly sliced green onions. 

#### 3. Fold the Dressing
Pour the seasoned cream cheese and mayo dressing over the chicken mixture. Use a silicone spatula to gently fold everything together until every shred of chicken, sweet grape, and crisp bit of celery is evenly coated.

#### 4. Add the Pecans & Finish
Gently fold in the 1/2 cup of roughly chopped candied pecans right at the very end to keep them crunchy. Taste and adjust with an extra crack of black pepper or pinch of salt if needed.

#### 5. Serve or Chill
Serve immediately piled high on buttery club crackers, tucked into warm croissants, or scooped over a bed of crisp greens. 

---

### Cook's Notes

* **The Cream Cheese Secret:** Softening the cream cheese thoroughly at room temperature is essential so it emulsifies smoothly into the mayonnaise without clumping.
* **Preserving Pecan Crunch:** Always fold the candied pecans in last, or sprinkle them directly over individual servings if you plan on storing leftovers in the fridge for several days.
