My name is Ted, and I live in South San Francisco.  I'm a patent attorney, but I'm also a husband, father, sports fanatic, and foodie.

![](/assets/images/about-pic-1.jpg){: width="500"}

In terms of food that I focus on in this page, I have a variety of tastes from my background in different parts of the world.  I spent my early days in the south near Little Rock, Arkansas.  Then I lived a good chunk of my adolescent and young adult life in the midwest near Milwaukee, Wisconsin.

My goal with this site is to share my experiences in the bay, as well as my favorite recipes, and also to feed my own urge to do some writing I enjoy.  If there is anything in particular that you would like to see on this page connect with me on my Instagram account listed below and let me know!
